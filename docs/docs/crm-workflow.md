# CRM Workflow — AthletIQ

## Purpose
To organize partnerships, outreach, and lead tracking.

---

## Pipeline Stages

### Lead Identified
Potential contact added.

### Contacted
Initial message sent.

### Engaged
Conversation active.

### Negotiation
Partnership discussion ongoing.

### Closed
Partnership confirmed.

### Lost
No response or opportunity declined.

---

## Workflow Rules
- Every lead must have a status
- Notes should be updated after interactions
- Outreach progress must be tracked weekly
